Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS), also known as Myalgic Encephalomyelitis (ME/CFS)

ME/CFS is a complex, long-term illness. Diagnosis requires three core symptoms: a substantial reduction in the ability to engage in pre-illness activities lasting more than six months and accompanied by profound fatigue, post-exertional malaise, and unrefreshing sleep. At least one of cognitive impairment or orthostatic intolerance must also be present.

Post-exertional malaise is the worsening of symptoms after even minor physical or mental exertion, often delayed by 12 to 48 hours.

There is no cure. Management focuses on pacing to stay within the patient's energy envelope, treating sleep problems and pain, and addressing orthostatic intolerance. Graded exercise therapy is no longer recommended because it can worsen symptoms.
